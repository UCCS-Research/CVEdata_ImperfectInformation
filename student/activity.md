# Activity

You are an engineer preparing a dependency-maintenance recommendation. Read the supplied records in `data/cases.json` or `data/cases.csv`. Use the decision memo to connect your claim, evidence, uncertainty, and next action. Record which fields support your reasoning. A record reports information about software; you have not been given a project dependency inventory.

1. Individually inspect A1 and D1 and write initial memos.
2. In pairs, examine B1-B3 and C1-C3. Each partner challenges an inference or offers an alternative interpretation. Respond using the supplied fields.
3. Revisit one memo. Explain any change in the recommendation, or why retaining it is justified.
4. Identify what evidence you would need next and how it would affect the recommendation.

For Case C, inspect the exact grouping rule in `analysis/original_unique_view.sql`. Describe the entity being counted and explain what trimming whitespace would change. The task can be completed without executing SQL.

The instructor may supply an unfamiliar record from an existing, separately held collection for independent practice. No transfer record or learner outcome is included in this release.
