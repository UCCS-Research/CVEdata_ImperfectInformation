# Activity

Read `data/cases.json` or `data/cases.csv`, the four memo prompts, and `student/decision_briefs.md`. A-D use unrelated components in separate constructed project contexts. The records preserve snapshot values; the briefs supply hypothetical decisions.

1. Individually write a Case D adoption memo; contrast the first evidence needed in Case A.
2. In pairs, analyze Cases B and C. Each partner challenges an inference; the other responds with supporting fields and a reasoned action.
3. Discuss D+, then revise one memo or explain why the original recommendation remains justified.
4. Complete the two independent tasks only when the instructor distributes them. Do not read instructor guidance beforehand.

Your memo should state a supported claim, interpret its evidence, explain decision-relevant uncertainty or evidence sufficiency, and justify the next action. Explain what finding would change the recommendation. More caution or a longer response does not automatically earn more credit.

Case C can be completed by inspecting `analysis/original_unique_view.sql` without executing SQL. Distinguish exact groups, groups after trimming, and distinct CVEs.
