# Vocabulary

- CVE: an identifier for a publicly disclosed vulnerability; its presence alone does not establish a particular project's exposure.
- Package ecosystem: the distribution/package-management context, such as PyPI or npm. Names alone may be ambiguous across contexts.
- Dependency coordinate: information that identifies the component and version being considered or used.
- Affected range: versions reported as affected. `<2.32.4` means versions below 2.32.4; a version outside a range is not thereby free of other risks.
- Project applicability: whether report facts apply to the actual component, version, and relevant use conditions.
- Extracted metadata: stored fields produced from a source through processing; they may require corroboration.
- Corroboration: evidence supporting a claim beyond repeating the same extracted value.
- Grouping rule: the key used to combine/select records. Counting package-version groups differs from counting CVEs or applicable vulnerabilities.
- Resolving a specific concern: showing why the available evidence settles that concern without claiming overall security.
