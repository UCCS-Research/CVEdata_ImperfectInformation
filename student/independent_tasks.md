# Independent practice: distribute after instruction

These records are reserved from Cases A-D. Work alone without case-specific hints or peer help. For each task, recommend an action and justify it from the supplied evidence. State the conclusion you can support and the limits of that conclusion. Project briefs are hypothetical; record values remain unchanged in `data/independent_records.json`.

## T1
An image-processing integration team receives T1 through a dashboard. A teammate proposes removing a dependency based on the extracted package field. No inventory or deployment configuration is supplied. Should that field drive the change? Recommend a next action and explain what evidence could change it.

## T2
A team considering langgraph for a service receives T2. Its candidate version and treatment of incoming data have not been selected. Recommend what to do with this report before acceptance. Explain the role and limits of the stored fields.

The two tasks are supplied practice candidates. They have not been validated as equivalent pre/post forms or as research measures. Instructors must keep them unseen until administration to use them for independent assessment.
