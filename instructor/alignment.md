# Alignment of goals, tasks, assessment, and evaluation

| Goal | Practice | Student output | Rubric | Evaluation observation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| State a supported, bounded claim | A-D; D+ | Scope/conditions of a claim | Claim scope | Unsupported applicability claims; bounded closure |
| Interpret supporting fields and their provenance | A extraction; B names; C grouping; D range/snippet | Field-to-claim explanation | Evidence trace | Limitation or sufficiency identified and connected to project |
| Explain the decision consequence of a limitation | A mapping; B identity; C loss of CVE references; D missing version/use | Relevant uncertainty and what finding matters | Uncertainty | Connection between evidence limitation and decision |
| Justify a targeted action or bounded closure | A-D; D+ | Action and reason; evidence that would change it | Next action | Targeted action or justified closure |

Peer critique/revision is a proposed instructional mechanism; revised memos provide process evidence, not an independent transfer measure. T1/T2 reserve extraction and applicability contrasts for independent classroom practice. They do not supply all evaluation strata: formal research must add unseen forms, including naming/grouping contrasts and sufficient-evidence briefs. No claim of validated form equivalence is made.
